Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Welcome to ToolNest. We provide free online tools for everyday tasks, including image compression, image resizing, image format conversion and QR code generation.

1. Information We Collect

ToolNest's basic image tools process files directly in your web browser. We do not intentionally upload or store your selected images on our servers.

2. How Your Files Are Processed

Images selected for compression, resizing or conversion are processed locally in your browser whenever possible. You should avoid uploading private or sensitive information to any online service.

3. QR Codes

Text or links entered into the QR generator are used to create the QR code in your browser. ToolNest does not intentionally store the text you enter.

4. Cookies

ToolNest does not require cookies for the basic operation of its tools. Third-party services, analytics or advertising providers may use cookies if they are added to the website in the future.

5. Third-Party Services

Some website features may use third-party libraries or services. Those services may have their own privacy policies and terms.

6. Children's Privacy

ToolNest is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website or services change. The updated version will be posted on this page.

8. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the ToolNest website owner through the contact method provided on the website.
